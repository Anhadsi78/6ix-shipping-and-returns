Shipping and returns on 6ix Merchandise tribute tees and hoodies

6ix Merchandise is a Toronto streetwear tribute label: the graphics are original artwork designed in the city, not official artist-licensed merchandise. If you are ordering a tee or hoodie, the practical questions are usually shipping and what happens if the size is wrong.

Shipping

Shipping is free on every order, worldwide. The site says orders are delivered in 5 to 9 days. Payment options listed on the store include Apple Pay and credit or debit cards.

Returns

There is a 14-day return window for items that are unworn and still have their tags. If you need a different size, keep the piece in that state and use the store's return path rather than wearing it first.

What you can order

The shop sells graphic t-shirts, hoodies and other streetwear with Toronto-designed artwork, including tribute collections inspired by music culture, sport and anime, plus made-on-demand pieces in the Design Studio. Start from the collections if you already know the shelf you want, or from the homepage if you are browsing.
